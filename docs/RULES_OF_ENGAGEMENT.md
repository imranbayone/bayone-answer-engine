# Rules of Engagement

These rules govern every draft the agent produces and every answer an executive posts. They exist to protect two things at once: BayOne's credibility on Reddit, and BayOne's citation-worthiness to AI engines. Both depend on answers being genuinely useful, not promotional.

## The Core Rule

**Be helpful first. Link last, and rarely.**

Most answers should carry no link to BayOne at all. Only include one when it is genuinely the best resource for the specific question asked — not because it's convenient to mention.

## Disclosure

Every posting account belongs to a real, named BayOne executive, with their role stated in their Reddit bio. No anonymous accounts. No ghostwriting under a persona that isn't the actual person posting. If a reasonable reader would want to know the answer is coming from someone at a vendor in this space, that fact should already be visible on the account, not hidden and then revealed only if asked.

## What Makes a Good Answer

- **Specific and data-backed.** Cite a real number, a named framework, or a concrete example rather than general reassurance.
- **Takes a position.** "It depends" answers do not get cited by AI engines. If the honest answer is "it depends," explain what it depends on and then still give a recommendation.
- **Answers the question asked.** Not a redirect toward a BayOne service page dressed up as an answer.
- **Reads like a person, not a brand.** Matches the tone and depth of the subreddit it's posted in.

## BayOne Style Rules (apply to every draft)

- No em dashes
- American English spellings
- Never refer to coworkers or employees as "family"
- Avoid AI-writing patterns: antithetical constructions ("it's not just X, it's Y"), mirrored parallel phrasing, fragmented punchy closers
- Concise, professional, warm — not corporate-stiff, not overly casual

## What the Agent Should Never Do

- Never post automatically, under any condition
- Never suggest a link to a BayOne asset unless it is the single best answer to the specific question
- Never draft an answer that omits a genuine tradeoff or downside to make BayOne look better
- Never draft content implying an anonymous or third-party endorsement

## Subreddit-Specific Rules

Before posting for the first time in a new subreddit, the executive should check that subreddit's own rules (most have a sidebar or pinned post) for restrictions on self-promotion, vendor mentions, or account-age requirements. This is a two-minute check that avoids a removed post or a ban.

## Account Warm-Up

Newly created executive accounts should have some organic history (a few comments, some upvoting activity) before their first substantive answer. A brand-new account posting a long, polished, data-backed answer on day one reads as suspicious both to moderators and to other readers. Two to three weeks of light, genuine activity before the pilot's first real answers is the target.
